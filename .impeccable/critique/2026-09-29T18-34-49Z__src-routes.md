## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM: world fully authored in 3 places (home hero, running timer, held). ~80 % of screens (first launch, forms, history, stats, calendar, settings, protocol, goal, stopped timer) fall back to a generic dark shadcn look. One design system, not yet one world: "a lit lobby plus a back office". Seams between the 7 lanes show in navigation chrome, hand-rolled craving type, card shells.
Deterministic: CLI 4 advisory findings (2 arbitrary font sizes in craving, 2 hard-coded haze stops `#3a1a10` `#1c1011`), all true. Browser: dark-glow (Envie) and radial-halo (haze) are the world's own devices — deliberate; cramped-padding, tight-leading, text-overflow, text-occlusion — false positives (fixed-height buttons, sr-only tables); layout-transition on ProgressBar true but gated by motion-reduce.

## What's working

1. Home hero and timer faithful to the mock: digit-aware figure size, stepped count-up with sr-only final value, drift under motion-safe only, haze opacities from a contrast test (timer text ≥6.4:1 mid-drift).
2. Colour roles disciplined: gradient = Envie only; alert only for errors/delete; nothing lapse-related red. Every token pair clears AA.
3. Selection language unified: cream fill = selected everywhere.
